## 1. 🎯 Objective

To create an impactful one-page financial dashboard that captures the essence of ITC Limited’s performance from FY2020 to FY2024. By leveraging advanced financial concepts like ratio analysis, CAGR, and enterprise valuation, this project provides a comprehensive yet concise summary of ITC's sales growth, profitability, valuation multiples, and strategic direction. The aim is to transform complex financial data into a clear, engaging visual narrative that offers actionable insights for business decision-making.

## 🔍 Key Takeaways from the Report
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/aaed0329-a795-4291-ba63-35e0365cac3a)


### 🏷 Sales & Growth
- **FY24 Revenue:** ₹70,866 Cr  
- **5-Year CAGR:** 9.3%, reflecting steady post-COVID recovery  

### 💰 Profitability
- **EBITDA Margin:** ~37%  
- **Net Margin:** 28.87%  
- **Gross Margin:** 52.8%  
- Strong margins indicate **efficient operations and cost control**  

### 📊 Valuation Multiples
- **P/E Ratio:** 24.5x  
- **EV/EBITDA:** 18.85x  
- **EV/Sales:** 6.98x  
- Suggests **moderate-to-premium market valuation**  

### 📈 Capital Efficiency
- **ROE:** 27.46%  
- **ROCE:** 37.73%  
- Indicates **strong returns on shareholder and total capital**  

### 💸 Shareholder Returns
- **EPS (FY24):** ₹16.39  
- **Dividend/Share:** ₹13.75  
- **Payout Ratio:** 83.9%  
- Consistent income distribution with **retained earnings for reinvestment**  

### 🔥 Strategic Moves
- **Acquisitions:**  
  - **24 Mantra Organic** (Expanding FMCG portfolio)  
  - **Aditya Birla’s Pulp & Paper Unit** (Vertical integration strategy)  
- Signals **diversification into FMCG and strengthened supply chain control**
